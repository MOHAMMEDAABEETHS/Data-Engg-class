PART - X
--------




Product can have five colors. 				---  Array (ordered collection of similar simple values)

Customer has one residential address.			---  Embedded Document (one-to-one relationship bounded to the parent)

Order contains multiple products.			---  Array of Embedded Documents (items subset tightly scoped to the order)

Product has RAM, storage and processor information.	---  Embedded Document (attributes describing a single entity directly)

Customer can have thousands of orders over many years.	---  Separate Collection / References (avoids document growth limits / 16MB ceiling)

Product can have millions of customer reviews.		---  Separate Collection / References (unbounded growth)

Employee has first name and last name.			---  Embedded Document / Simple Field

Product has multiple tags.				---  Array (flexible multi-valued attributes)
